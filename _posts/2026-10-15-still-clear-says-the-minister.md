*–on Bali's fuel queues, and a political scientist attempting arithmetic*

<p class="post-tags"><span class="tag">#data</span> <span class="tag">#energy</span> <span class="tag">#Bali</span></p>

My career as a queue analyst began in 2010, when I counted dusty motorbikes outside a bike wash in Jogja for a newspaper column. It has not advanced much since. This week the queues came back, this time for Pertalite in Bali: lines snaked into the streets around stations, and some drivers waited more than an hour. In Jembrana alone, [fourteen stations were hit](https://www.detik.com/bali/bisnis/d-8704008/bali-krisis-bbm-antrean-horor-di-mana-mana){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, some with neither Pertalite nor Pertamax left. Pertamina has apologised, blaming a tanker whose [anchor problem delayed unloading](https://pasardana.id/news/2026/10/14/bos-pertamina-minta-maaf-dan-beberkan-bbm-langka-di-bali){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} on 12 October from 6:30 in the morning to 10 at night, and is sending [26,200 kilolitres in three deliveries](https://www.jawapos.com/energi/2610130122/atasi-kelangkaan-bbm-di-bali-pertamina-datangkan-20500-kl-bensin){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} between 12 and 16 October.

Energy Minister Bahlil Lahadalia's reading is reassuring. National stock, he said on 14 October, [stands at 16 to 18 days](https://www.cnbcindonesia.com/news/20261014185307-4-776278/antrean-bbm-mengular-di-bali-bahlil-pastikan-stok-18-hari-aman/amp){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, "so it is still clear." The trouble in Bali is distribution, not supply.

On Bali itself, he is probably right. One late tanker, an island that burns [some 2,200 kilolitres of Pertalite a day](https://www.jawapos.com/energi/2610130122/atasi-kelangkaan-bbm-di-bali-pertamina-datangkan-20500-kl-bensin){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, and a bout of panic buying are enough to produce the scenes we saw. I say this with the full authority of someone who has never unloaded anything heavier than a box of books. The minister's own numbers, however, deserve a closer look.

Why would 16 days count as clear?

I read the oil and gas directorate's 224-page performance report so that you don't have to. It is not Pramoedya.<sup id="fnref:pram"><a href="#fn:pram" class="footnote" rel="footnote" role="doc-noteref">1</a></sup> But it does tell two stories. For years the buffer [hovered between 20 and 23 days](https://www.esdm.go.id/assets/media/content/content-laporan-kinerja-ditjen-minyak-dan-gas-bumi-tahun-2025.pdf){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}; this year it has slipped to 16 to 20, the lowest since at least 2019. Even the old level sat just under the ceiling: storage [holds at most 25 days](https://industri.kontan.co.id/news/bahlil-klarifikasi-stok-bbm-tinggal-20-hari-pemerintah-pastikan-pasokan-aman){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, against the 90 [expected of IEA members](https://www.iea.org/about/oil-security-and-emergency-response){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}. And the record itself is thin: one year-end number a year, [tucked into that performance report](https://www.esdm.go.id/assets/media/content/content-laporan-kinerja-ditjen-minyak-dan-gas-bumi-tahun-2025.pdf){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, and in between, only what officials happen to mention at press conferences.

Cooking gas is thinner still: LPG cover [fell to 14.5 days at the end of 2025](https://www.esdm.go.id/assets/media/content/content-laporan-kinerja-ditjen-minyak-dan-gas-bumi-tahun-2025.pdf){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, from 17.3 two years earlier.

The buffer matters because Indonesia no longer fuels itself. Oil output [averaged about 570,000 barrels a day](https://www.antaranews.com/berita/5592216/realisasi-produksi-minyak-kondensat-dan-ngl-tercatat-5762-mbopd){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} in January–May, below the 610,000 target. In 2025 the country needed 84.6 million kilolitres of fuel and [imported 28.9 million of it](https://www.esdm.go.id/assets/media/content/content-laporan-kinerja-ditjen-minyak-dan-gas-bumi-tahun-2025.pdf){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} outright, on top of the crude its refineries buy abroad. Bahlil says Indonesia now has [long-term contracts outside the Middle East](https://www.rctiplus.com/news/detail/terkini/5407826/ri-tak-lagi-bergantung-pada-minyak-timur-tengah--bahlil--sudah-kontrak-dengan-negara-lain){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, which once supplied [a fifth to a quarter of our crude imports](https://industri.kontan.co.id/news/porsi-impor-minyak-ri-dari-timur-tengah-25-pemerintah-siapkan-skenario-ini){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}. But barrels can be rerouted; prices cannot. Tanker attacks around Hormuz have kept Brent above $100: it [closed at $106.65 on 12 October](https://www.rigzone.com/news/wire/brent_tops_106_as_supply_risks_intensify-12-oct-2026-184830-article/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}. In other words, a thin buffer sits at the end of an expensive and exposed supply line. That is not a distribution problem.

None of this means Indonesia is about to run dry. It does mean that the government's confidence rests on figures the public cannot check. The figures also drift from one interview to the next, which, *aduh*, is unkind to those of us who can only do sums with the numbers we are given. In early March the minister was reported as saying 20 days, then [clarified that it was 23](https://industri.kontan.co.id/news/bahlil-klarifikasi-stok-bbm-tinggal-20-hari-pemerintah-pastikan-pasokan-aman){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}. On 14 October, the day he said 16 to 18, his own director-general of oil and gas [put it at 18 to 20](https://www.suara.com/bisnis/2026/10/14/193400/banyak-antrean-di-berbagai-daerah-cadangan-stok-bbm-aman){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, although a 2020 BPH Migas regulation [requires at least 23](https://www.bphmigas.go.id/bph-migas-pantau-pendistribusian-dan-cadangan-operasional-bbm-badan-usaha/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}. By the regulator's own standard, today's figure falls short. Publishing weekly cover by product and by region would cost almost nothing, and it would let the public judge whether "clear" means clear.

Public confidence is a reserve too, and unlike fuel, it cannot be shipped in by tanker. The minister would do well to publish the numbers before the next queue forms, not after. Until then, I shall return to what I do best: counting motorbikes.
